# PRODUCT.md — NutriAI

## 1. Product Truth
- **Product:** NutriAI
- **Mission:** High-performance, mobile-first nutrition intelligence platform designed to eliminate friction in meal decisions and macro tracking through the Swipe Machine & Matchup Showdown.
- **Core User:** Health-conscious individuals, fitness enthusiasts, busy developers and professionals who want exact nutritional alignment without tedious manual food entry.
- **Primary Use Scene:** Smartphone in hand, in the kitchen (holding a phone while checking the fridge) or on-the-go at lunch/dinner.

## 2. Experience Invariants
- **Ergonomics:** True one-hand thumb-zone navigation. Bottom navigation, gesture-driven bottom sheets, oversized touch targets (min 48px), zero hover dependencies.
- **Speed & Tactile Feel:** 60 FPS spring physics, instant local state updates, background card pre-fetching (zero spinner wait during meal discovery).
- **Anti-Slop Voice:** Professional, energetic, culinary-accurate, direct, zero generic placeholder emojis or gimmicky glows.
